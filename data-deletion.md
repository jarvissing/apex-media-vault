# User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Apex Survival Auto Publisher does not store personal data or account information of public users on external servers.

If you wish to request deletion of any interaction data or activities associated with Apex Survival:
1. Go to your Facebook Settings & Privacy > Settings.
2. Select 'Apps and Websites' to view apps connected to your Facebook account.
3. Find 'Apex Survival Auto Publisher' and click 'Remove'.
4. To request permanent deletion of any records or comments, please open an issue at: https://github.com/jarvissing/apex-media-vault/issues or contact the page administrator. We will process and confirm deletion within 48 hours.
