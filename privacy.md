# Privacy Policy for Apex Survival Auto Publisher

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Apex Survival Auto Publisher is an internal automation tool operated by Apex Survival to publish curated survival documentary videos and reels to YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

## 1. Information Collected
Apex Survival Auto Publisher does not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal data, cookies, or user profile information from public users or viewers.

## 2. API Usage & Platform Permissions
The application accesses platform APIs (Meta Graph API, YouTube Data API) strictly through authorized developer tokens to upload video media, titles, and descriptions directly to our official social media channels.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our services interact with YouTube (Google) and Meta (Facebook & Instagram). We adhere to the respective developer platform policies and data usage agreements.

## 4. Contact Us
For any privacy questions or inquiries regarding our automated publishing tools, please contact us via our official page: https://github.com/jarvissing/apex-media-vault
